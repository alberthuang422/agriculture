# HEADLINES - Quarterly Market Outlook - NOVEMBER 2025

## World Sugar Market

- First revision of the 2025/26 moves global balance to a surplus of 1.625 mln tonnes
- Latest 2024/25 revision reduces global deficit to 2.916 mln tonnes from 4.879 mln tonnes
- Global consumption forecast to rise 0.56% to 180.142 mln tonnes in 2025/26
- Updates show consumption hit a record of 181.207 mln tonnes in 2023/24
- Stock-to-use ratio at 52.74%, a 9-year low, in 2025/26 while the alternative ISO ending-stock calculation points to a 15-year low

## Market Price Update

- Average monthly ISA prices in November (to 11th) below USD14 cents/lb and a 57-month low
- The ISO price view is neutral over the next three months given projected surplus, production seasonality and ample export availability
- Brent crude averaged USD70.95/barrel in July, down from USD79.21/barrel in January amid OPEC+ supply increases
- Corn prices fell to one-year low at USD4.02/bushel in July on favourable US weather, bumper Brazilian harvest, and trade tensions
- Coffee prices surged to USD3.70/lb in August after falling to nine-month low of USD2.92/lb in July
- US Dollar weakened against other currencies and the dollar index basket
- Most major sugar markets experienced price decreases in May-July as USD-denominated global raw sugar prices declined 6%
- Brazilian crystal sugar prices fell to three-year lows

## World Fuel Ethanol Market

- World ethanol production forecast at 120.6 bln litres in 2025, up 1.7% from 2024
- Global consumption projected at 120.5 bln litres in 2025, up 2.6% from 2024
- US production expected to reach record 61.5 bln litres in 2025
- Brazilian ethanol production forecast at 32.7 bln litres in 2025, down from 33.7 bln litres as mills favour sugar production
- India's production projected to surge to 9.5 bln litres in 2025, up from 7.2 bln litres, supporting 20% blending targets

## Co Product Markets

### Molasses
- Little upward momentum for prices over coming months
- EU 2025/26 beet molasses output higher than initially thought
- Cane molasses market is likely to be better supplied in 2026
- Both EU and US importing less in 2025
- Key importers in Asia show fewer shipments in first three quarters of 2025

### Cogeneration
- Brazil: Calendar year bagasse electricity sales at 7,076 GWh up to June 2025, down 10.4% year-over-year
- India: Bagasse power output at 8,459 GWh for 2024/25, down 15.3% to multi-season low

### Bioplastics
- UN Global Plastics Treaty negotiations conclude without agreement after sixth round, delaying regulatory certainty
- Thailand advancing bio-hub strategy with NatureWorks securing USD350 mln for 75,000-tonne PLA plant using sugarcane feedstock

## Alternative Sweeteners

- Shrinking US demand and fewer exports to Mexico weigh on 2026 US HFCS production
- US net corn sweetener costs drift lower from February 2025 high
- Forecast recovery in EU isoglucose production in 2025/26
- A shift toward plant-based and blended sweeteners?
- US consumers embracing sugar, but less of it.
- Advanced sweetening systems can overcome technical, sustainability and cost barriers
- Coca Cola sees gains in low- and no-sugar carbonated soft-drinks and high-protein drinks from consumers using GLP-1 drugs
- Campaign to ban Aspartame in Europe and US continues
- Another sweet protein achieves GRAS status in the US
