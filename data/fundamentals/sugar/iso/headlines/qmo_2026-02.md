# HEADLINES - Quarterly Market Outlook - FEBRUARY 2026

## World Sugar Market

- Second revision of the 2025/26 moves global balance to a surplus of 1.218 mln tonnes
- Latest 2024/25 update shifts global deficit to 3.464 mln tonnes from 2.916 mln tonnes
- Global consumption forecast to rise 0.31% to 180.069 mln tonnes in 2025/26
- Trade surplus of 1.102 mln tonnes in 2025/26 is in line with production / consumption balance
- Stock-to-use ratio at 51.81% in 2025/26 while the alternative ISO ending-stock calculation points to a 15-year low

## Market Price Update

- Average monthly ISA prices in February (up to the 20th) below USD14 cents/lb and 5-year low
- The ISO price view is neutral over the next three months with the modest surplus that is projected. However, tight stocks and lower than expected production for India and Thailand, together with uncertainty around CS Brazil sugar allocation, could change the outlook
- Brent crude averaged USD64.59/barrel in January, up 4.6% from December's post-2021 low, as geopolitical supply risks intensified
- Corn prices post first monthly decline since August 2025 on raised global production forecasts and record output in China and the US
- Coffee prices retreated to a five-month low in January
- Cocoa plunged to a two-year low amid consecutive supply surplus forecasts
- US Dollar weakened further, with its 10% depreciation in 2025 marking the worst annual performance against major trading partners in over five decades
- Most major sugar markets experienced price decreases between Nov–Jan despite the ISA Daily Price rising modestly
- Mexican estándar sugar prices surged 15%

## World Fuel Ethanol Market

- World ethanol production estimated at 122.9 bln litres in 2025, from 119.2 bln litres in 2024; forecast to rise further to 127.7 bln litres in 2026
- Global consumption estimated at 122.7 bln litres in 2025, from 117.8 bln litres in 2024; projected to reach 125.3 bln litres in 2026
- US production reached record 62.5 bln litres in 2025; exports at an all-time high of 8.3 bln litres
- Brazil fuel ethanol production at 33.2 bln litres in 2025; forecast to rise to 36.3 bln litres in 2026
- India's production reaches a record 10.4 bln litres in 2025; projected to reach 12 bln litres in 2026
- EU reversed its outright 2035 ban on internal combustion engine vehicles

## Co Product Markets

- Global exportable supplies of molasses expected to increase in 2026
- Bigger crops in key exporters, and two major policy decisions, put downward pressure on cane molasses prices
- Beet molasses will be tighter in 2025/26 given the lower crop in Europe
- Molasses exports from nine key origins down 2% in 2025
- Australia, Indonesia, and Thailand are largest Asian/Pacific region exporters in 2025
- US imports less in 2025, but extra-EU imports recover
- Aggregate imports by five key Asian destinations down 20% in 2025
- Brazil: bagasse-based electricity sales totalled 20,409 GWh in 2025, down 3.8% on previous year
- India: Bagasse power output in 2025/26 (October–September) at highest level since 2022/23
- Global bioplastics production capacity projected to double between 2025 and 2030
- EU Bioeconomy Strategy introduces bio-based content targets for bio-based polymers

## Alternative Sweeteners

- Forecast further contraction in US offtake and expected export losses to Mexico weigh on 2026 US HFCS outlook
- US net corn sweetener costs rise in 2025
- Annual US HFCS price negotiations saw price roll-over for 2026
- Modest recovery in EU isoglucose production forecast in 2025/26
- Biotechnology boosting production of Monkfruit sweetener
- New biosynthetic method in development to produce rare sugar Tagatose
- Sugar reduction technology advancing
