## World Sugar Market

- The ISO's third revision moves the 2025/26 global balance to a surplus of 2.244 mln tonnes
- Latest 2024/25 update narrows the global deficit to 3.196 mln tonnes from 3.464 mln tonnes
- Global consumption forecast to rise 0.39% to 179.760 mln tonnes in 2025/26
- Trade surplus of 0.900 mln tonnes in 2025/26 is smaller than the production / consumption balance, pointing to some stock accumulations
- Stock-to-use ratio at 44.15% in 2025/26, using revised methodology, fractionally above last season's 15-year low

## Market Price Update

- Average monthly ISA prices in May (up to 12th) of USD 15.54 cents/lb is a season-to-date high.
- The ISO price view is neutral over the next three months with CS Brazil exports the main feature. Precautionary stock building together with uncertainty around CS Brazil sugar allocation, could change the outlook
- Persian Gulf conflict drives sharp re-pricing of energy and fertiliser markets from late February
- Brent crude averaged USD102.81/barrel in April, up sharply from the mid-USD70s in January
- Middle East FOB granular urea up 54% in March to around USD725/tonne, highest since April 2022
- US Dollar broadly stable this quarter; BRL and CNY appreciated further
- Indian Rupee hit an all-time low of 95.6/USD in mid-May
- ISA Daily Price up 3% February–April, but domestic markets diverged
- Brazilian crystal prices broadly stable; EU and Mexican prices eased; Russian prices surged 12%

## World Fuel Ethanol Market

- World ethanol production at 123.1 bln litres in 2025, up 3.3%; forecast at 129.4 bln litres in 2026
- Global consumption at 122.9 bln litres in 2025, up 4.4%; projected 126.9 bln litres in 2026
- Persian Gulf conflict accelerates biofuel policy momentum globally from late February
- Brazil, India and the EU eye next step up in blends, towards E32, E25 and E20 respectively
- Falling sugar prices tilt CS Brazil's cane allocation towards ethanol in 2026/27
- Corn ethanol displaces cane-based supply in Brazil and India as the feedstock pivot deepens
- New mandates in Indonesia, Vietnam, Nepal, Ukraine and Zimbabwe broaden the demand base

## Co Product Markets

### Molasses
- Exportable molasses supplies set to increase in 2026
- Larger crops in key exporters, combined with major policy shifts (India's removal of its prohibitive export tax, the Philippines' import ban and Pakistan's loss of GSP status in the EU) exert downward pressure on cane molasses prices
- Beet molasses supplies will be tighter due to lower yields in Europe
- Australia, Indonesia, and Thailand to remain the leading exporters in Asian/Pacific region in 2026
- US imports stable in 2026 but extra-EU imports likely boosted by reduced beet molasses production
- Aggregate imports by five key Asian buyers down 20% in 2025 and slumped in first quarter of 2026 following Philippines' imports ban

### Cogeneration
- Brazil: Q1 2026 bagasse electricity sales up 35% on Q1 2025 at 975.3 GWh
- India: Q1 2026 bagasse generation at 4,299 GWh, down 6.2% and the lowest Q1 since 2017

### Bioplastics
- UN Plastics Treaty: INC-5.3 procedural only; INC-5.4 pushed to late 2026 or early 2027
- Braskem launches first sugarcane-derived bio-PE grades for food-contact and pharma packaging
- EU Council endorses new Bioeconomy Strategy
- India: Balrampur Bioyug's 80,000 tonne/year PLA facility on track for October 2026 commissioning

## Alternative Sweeteners

- Forecast declines in US HFCS demand and reduced exports to Mexico continue to weigh on 2026 US production
- US net corn sweetener costs declined in first four months of 2026
- US HFCS annual price negotiations resulted in a 2026 roll-over
- No change in EU isoglucose production in 2025/26
- Biotechnology accelerating production of sweet proteins via precision fermentation
- Long-running debate over artificial sweeteners intensifies with new studies raising fresh health concerns

## WTO Update

- MC14 delivered no negotiated outcomes on agriculture - public stocking the core blockage, cotton negotiations stalled completely and no progress made on domestic support, special safeguard mechanism, export restrictions or market access
- Post MC14 agriculture negotiations remain stalled
