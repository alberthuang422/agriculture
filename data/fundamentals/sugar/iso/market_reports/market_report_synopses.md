## Market Report - November 2025
- URL: https://isosugar.org/publication/432/market-report---november-2025
- The annual ISO London meetings, held at the end of November and into early December, took place against the backdrop of prices falling to a 57-month low. Although part of October's sharp decline was recovered in the latter half of November, prices remain significantly below September levels. November saw the tailing off of production in CS Brazil. Meanwhile, confirmation of global beet sugar production totals as well as the opening figures for cane sugar output in South Asia give a new focus for the market and signals the definitive start of the 2025/26 season.

## Market Report - December 2025
- URL: https://isosugar.org/publication/433/market-report---december-2025
- A modest recovery in November was followed by a low-volume, range-bound market in December, reflecting the continued dominance of speculators who maintained short positions throughout 2025. December saw a continued tailing off in Centre-South (CS) Brazil production while reports from the North-Northeast (NNE) region indicated higher diversion to ethanol and potential reductions in cane availability. Thailand's production is running behind schedule, and Russia's beet processing industry struggled with lower sucrose recovery rates. The EU provided a brighter note, with beet yields exceeding expectations.

## Market Report - August 2026
- URL: https://www.isosugar.org/publication/462/market-report---august-2026
- World raw sugar prices rallied strongly in August, with October futures rising from around USD15 cents/lb at the start of the month to USD17.8 cents/lb by August 31st. Prices have risen above USD18 cents/lb in the first few days of September. The ISA Daily Raw Sugar Price averaged USD17.4 cents/lb in August, a USD2.0 cents/lb or 13% increase from the July average.
